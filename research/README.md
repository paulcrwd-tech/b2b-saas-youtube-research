# Research: YouTube Content Strategy for B2B SaaS

## Topic
This research focuses on how SaaS founders/CEOs build a YouTube channel as an
organic growth engine for their B2B SaaS product — from content strategy and
upload consistency, to how their personal brand on YouTube converts into a
customer/coaching/community pipeline.

## Why This Topic
I picked this topic because video is where I want to build my skills, and YouTube is uniquely suited to B2B SaaS — it lets founders demonstrate expertise, build trust with a technical audience, and generate compounding organic leads. Studying founders who've actually built real businesses through YouTube (not just marketing agencies talking theory) gives me a practical playbook I can apply if I start my own SaaS someday

## Selected Experts
See full details in [`sources.md`](./sources.md). Summary: 10 SaaS founders/CEOs/investors
who genuinely use YouTube as a marketing channel for their own brand
(not agencies or consultants who only talk theory).
