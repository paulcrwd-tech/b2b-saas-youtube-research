# Sources — YouTube Content Strategy for B2B SaaS

List of 10 experts selected for this research. All are SaaS founders/CEOs/investors
who genuinely USE YouTube as a growth channel for their own product or brand —
not just consultants or agencies who only talk theory.

Selection criteria:
- Runs a real SaaS product/brand (not just a personal brand)
- Active YouTube channel (uploaded within the last 3 months as of July 2026)
- Content grounded in real experience (numbers, process, decisions), not generic tips
- Focused on B2B SaaS (not e-commerce, consumer apps, etc.)

## Methodology Notes
- All channels above were verified active as of July 2026 (video/post activity within the last 3 months)
- Priority: real practitioners (SaaS founder/CEO) over "marketing gurus" or agencies
- The top 6 (#1-6) are the most established, with the most historical content/data
- The last 4 (#7-10) add variety: early-stage building-in-public (#7), bootstrapped-exit voice (#8), enterprise SaaS (#9), consumer-to-B2B pivot (#10)
