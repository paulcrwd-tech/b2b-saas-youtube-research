# B2B SaaS YouTube Research

Research project on **YouTube Content Strategy for B2B SaaS** — part of a growth
marketing training program. The goal: identify genuinely credible practitioners
(not just theorists) and build a research base strong enough to support a real
playbook later.

## What This Is

This repo collects and organizes content from 10 SaaS founders/CEOs/investors who
actively use YouTube as a growth channel for their own B2B SaaS products — covering
their strategy, consistency, and how they convert an audience into customers,
coaching clients, or community members.
